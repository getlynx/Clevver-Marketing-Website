Article 2: Scope of Services

2.1 Platform Access

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Clevver grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to access and use the Platform Services during the Term for Customer's internal business purposes.

2.2 API Access

Customer shall receive full access to the Clevver RESTful API for programmatic storage, retrieval, tagging, and management of Assets. Batch processing capabilities are included as part of the API access.

2.3 Agent CLI Access

Customer shall receive access to the Clevver Agent CLI for AI agent automation workflows, including store, fetch, list, tag, and archive operations. Customer may deploy the Agent CLI across its Authorized Users' environments.

2.4 Core Platform Features

The Platform Services include the following core features:

  1. AES-256 encryption applied to all stored Assets by default
  2. Zero-knowledge architecture ensuring Clevver cannot access encrypted Asset contents
  3. Permanent, immutable storage of all Assets
  4. Tag-based organization with up to 100 tags per Asset
  5. Shareable Permanent URLs that never expire
  6. Free retrieval of stored Assets in perpetuity

2.5 Dedicated Account Manager

Clevver shall assign a dedicated account manager to Customer for onboarding, ongoing support, and custom integration assistance. The account manager shall serve as Customer's primary point of contact.

2.6 Custom Integration Support

Clevver shall provide reasonable custom integration support as described in Exhibit A, including assistance with API integration, Agent CLI deployment, and workflow configuration.

2.7 Modifications to Services

Clevver may modify the Platform Services at any time, provided that such modifications do not materially diminish the core functionality described in Section 2.4 during the Term. Clevver shall provide Customer with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any material changes to the Platform Services.

Article 4: Service Level Agreement

4.1 Uptime Commitment

Clevver commits to maintaining 99.9% monthly uptime for the API endpoints and Platform retrieval services ("Uptime Commitment"). Uptime is measured as the percentage of total minutes in a calendar month during which the Platform Services are operational and accessible. Platform status is publicly available at https://stats.uptimerobot.com/jBmX1iYk3l/.

4.2 Exclusions

The following events are excluded from the Uptime Commitment calculation:

  1. Scheduled maintenance windows, for which Clevver shall provide at least forty-eight (48) hours' prior written notice;
  2. Force majeure events as described in Article 14;
  3. Outages or performance degradation caused by Customer's equipment, software, or network connectivity;
  4. Failures of third-party services, internet service providers, or telecommunications infrastructure outside Clevver's reasonable control; and
  5. Periods during which Customer's access is suspended due to a breach of this Agreement or the ToS.

4.3 Service Level Credits

If Clevver fails to meet the Uptime Commitment in any calendar month, Customer shall be entitled to service credits as set forth in the following table:

Monthly Uptime Percentage Service Credit
99.0% – below Uptime Commitment 5% of applicable monthly Fees
95.0% – 98.99% 10% of applicable monthly Fees
Below 95.0% 15% of applicable monthly Fees

For one-time storage purchases, "applicable monthly Fees" shall be calculated as the total one-time Fees divided by the number of months in the Initial Term. For recurring Fees, the actual monthly amount shall apply.

4.4 Credit Request Procedure

Customer must submit a written request for service credits within thirty (30) days after the end of the month in which the Uptime Commitment was not met. Service credits shall be applied to future purchases or invoices and are not redeemable for cash refunds.

4.5 Maximum Credits

Total service credits in any twelve (12) month period shall not exceed 50% of total Fees paid or payable by Customer in such period.

4.6 Performance Targets

Clevver shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain API response times of less than 30 seconds at the 95th percentile for standard operations (store, fetch, list, tag).

4.7 Support Response Times

Clevver shall respond to Customer support requests in accordance with the following priority levels:

Priority Description Response Time
Critical Platform completely unavailable; all operations affected 1 hour
High Major feature degraded; significant business impact 4 hours
Medium Non-critical issue; workaround available 1 business day
Low General inquiry or feature request 2 business days

Article 8: Data Security and Encryption

8.1 Encryption at Rest

All Customer Data stored on the Platform is encrypted with AES-256 encryption at rest. Encryption is mandatory and cannot be disabled.

8.2 Zero-Knowledge Architecture

The Platform operates on a zero-knowledge architecture. Clevver cannot access, read, decrypt, or analyze the contents of Customer Data. Only authorized key holders designated by Customer can decrypt stored Assets.

8.3 Encryption in Transit

All data transmitted between Customer's systems and the Platform is protected via TLS/SSL encrypted connections.

8.4 Customer Security Obligations

Customer is solely responsible for:

  1. Managing and safeguarding all encryption keys, API credentials, and authentication tokens;
  2. Implementing appropriate access controls to limit Platform access to Authorized Users;
  3. Pre-encrypting any data requiring additional protection (including but not limited to protected health information, personally identifiable information, or other regulated data) before uploading to the Platform; and
  4. Maintaining the security of all systems and environments from which the API or Agent CLI is accessed.

8.5 Permanent Storage Acknowledgment

Customer acknowledges and agrees that all Customer Data stored on the Platform is stored permanently and immutably. Assets cannot be deleted, modified, or overwritten after upload. Customer shall exercise due care before uploading any Asset and shall not upload any data that may need to be erased or modified in the future.

8.6 Security Incident Notification

Clevver shall notify Customer in writing of any confirmed security incident that affects the Platform infrastructure within seventy-two (72) hours of Clevver's discovery of such incident. Such notice shall include, to the extent known: the nature of the incident, the data potentially affected, the corrective measures taken, and a point of contact for additional information.
