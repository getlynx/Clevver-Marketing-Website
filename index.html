Security

Enterprise-grade encryption, permanent by design

Every document is encrypted with AES-256 before it reaches storage. Combined with distributed redundancy and zero-knowledge architecture, your data remains protected and accessible for the long term.

AES-256 Encryption by Default

All stored data is automatically encrypted before it is written. Encryption is not optional or add-on — it is the standard for every document.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Clevver cannot access your encrypted documents. Your unique asset key serves as both the address and the decryption key, and it is never stored on our infrastructure.

Distributed Redundancy

Documents are stored across a geographically distributed network powered by distributed ledger technology, ensuring no single point of failure can compromise your data.
