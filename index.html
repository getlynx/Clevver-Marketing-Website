Security

Your AI work deserves real protection

Your saved files may contain sensitive data — project plans, personal notes, business strategy, private conversations. Clevver encrypts every file with AES-256 before storage. Combined with zero-knowledge architecture and distributed redundancy, your data stays private and accessible for the long term.

AES-256 Encryption by Default

Every file is automatically encrypted before it is stored. Encryption isn’t optional or add-on — it is the standard for every file you save.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Clevver cannot access your encrypted files. Your unique asset key serves as both the address and the decryption key, and it is never stored on our infrastructure.

Distributed Redundancy

Files are stored across a geographically distributed network powered by distributed ledger technology, ensuring no single point of failure can compromise your data.

No Corporate Gatekeepers

Your data isn't controlled by a single company or locked in a massive data center. Clevver is bootstrapped with zero VC funding — so there are no investors pressuring us to raise prices or change terms against your interest.

Energy-Efficient by Design

Our globally distributed storage network is designed to be highly energy efficient — no power-hungry centralized data centers. Better for your data and better for the climate.
