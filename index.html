Security

Encrypted storage for AI conversations and sensitive work

Your saved AI sessions may contain project plans, business strategy, private research, personal notes, or sensitive client data. Clevver encrypts every file with AES-256 before storage. Combined with zero-knowledge architecture and distributed redundancy, your data stays private and retrievable for the long term — even if you stop using Clevver for years.

AES-256 Encryption by Default

Every file is automatically encrypted before it touches our storage network. This isn't an optional add-on or premium feature. AES-256 encryption is the default for every file, every time — the same standard used by banks and government agencies.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Clevver cannot access your encrypted files. Your unique asset key works as both the file address and the decryption key, and we never store it on our infrastructure. If you're looking for end-to-end encrypted AI chat storage where even the provider can't read your data, this is it.

Distributed Redundancy Across Multiple Regions

Files are stored across a geographically distributed network powered by distributed ledger technology. No single server failure or data center outage can compromise your permanent archive of AI conversations and documents.
